Figure 7 recalculation method

Unit of analysis: reviewed paper.
For each paper, each institution/organization appearing in the author-affiliation information was counted once. Multiple authors, departments, or repeated affiliation identifiers referring to the same normalized institution within one paper generated one occurrence for that paper.
Institutional names were normalized where the source clearly used departmental/subunit variants of the same parent organization (e.g., TU Munich -> Technical University of Munich; IBM Quantum/IBM Japan -> IBM).
Figure 7 displays institutions represented in at least 3 of the 68 papers. The complete paper-by-paper audit and all normalized institution counts are provided in the accompanying CSV files.
